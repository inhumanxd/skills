---
name: grill-me
description: Interview the user relentlessly about a plan or design until reaching shared understanding, resolving each branch of the decision tree. Use when user wants to stress-test a plan, get grilled on their design, or mentions "grill me". Do NOT use when the plan should be challenged against domain docs, CONTEXT.md, or ADRs (use grill-with-docs), or for writing code.
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead. And ask as many related, non dependent questions as needed to reach a confident answer in one go. For each question, provide your recommended answer.
